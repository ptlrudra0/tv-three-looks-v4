# Your TV home in three looks

An interactive browser preview of three TV home-screen concepts. Open the site, tap **Modes** (or press `M`) to switch:

- **Artistic** - a curved app wheel with editorial typography.
- **Current** - an Apple TV-style home screen (v2): greeting + thin clock, icon pills, a rounded dock band for favourites, an all-apps row, a cinematic photo backdrop that shuffles every 25 seconds, and an **idle screen** (moon button, or after 60 s of no input) where the clock sits behind the photo's subject, iPhone lock screen style. Press any key to wake. Panels are simulated.
- **Harry Potter** - a wizarding-castle home screen at night, with the eight films as books on a shelf.

Everything runs in the browser as a demo: panels and app launches are simulated and nothing controls a real TV.

Keyboard: arrows to move, Enter to open, Esc to close, M for modes.
